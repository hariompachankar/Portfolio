CyberX

A full-stack Visitor Management System built for secure, efficient visitor operations in law-enforcement facilities.

Overview

CyberX replaces paper visitor logs with a role-based digital system for visitor check-in and check-out, live monitoring, QR badges, ID verification, and analytics.
